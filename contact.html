Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about our services.

How can I post a job on Al-Bina Careers?

You can post a job by clicking on the "Post a Job" button in the navigation bar. Fill out the form with your job details and submit. Our team will review and publish your job within 24 hours.

How can I apply for a job?

Browse our job listings, click on the job you're interested in, and click the "Apply Now" button. You can also create a profile to make applying faster and easier.

Is it free to post a job?

Yes, posting a job on Al-Bina Careers is completely free. We also offer premium features to boost your job visibility.

How do I create a job seeker account?

Click on "Sign Up" in the navigation bar, select "Job Seeker" as your account type, and fill out the registration form. It takes less than 2 minutes.

How long does it take to get a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call us directly.

Can I edit my job listing after posting?

Yes, you can edit your job listing at any time by logging into your employer account and accessing your job dashboard.
