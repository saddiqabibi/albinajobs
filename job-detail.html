Senior Software Engineer

Tech Solutions
Lahore, Pakistan Full Time PKR 150,000 - 250,000 Posted: Aug 15, 2026
Apply Now

Job Description

Tech Solutions is looking for a talented and experienced Senior Software Engineer to join our dynamic team in Lahore. As a Senior Software Engineer, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining high-quality software solutions that power our products and services.

You will work closely with cross-functional teams, including product managers, designers, and other engineers, to deliver scalable and innovative solutions. This is an excellent opportunity for a passionate engineer who wants to make a significant impact in a fast-growing technology company.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and Development: Design, develop, and maintain robust, scalable, and high-performance software applications using modern programming languages and frameworks.
  • Code Review: Conduct code reviews to ensure code quality, best practices, and adherence to coding standards.
  • Technical Leadership: Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior developers and team members.
  • Collaboration: Work closely with product managers, designers, and stakeholders to understand requirements and deliver solutions.
  • Problem Solving: Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues and performance bottlenecks.
  • Continuous Improvement: Stay updated with the latest technologies, trends, and best practices in software development.
  • Documentation: Create and maintain technical documentation for systems and applications.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Education: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.
  • Experience: 5+ years of professional experience in software development.
  • Technical Skills: Strong proficiency in Java, Python, or C#; experience with web frameworks (Spring Boot, Django, ASP.NET); knowledge of databases (SQL, NoSQL); experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) is a plus.
  • Soft Skills: Excellent communication, problem-solving, and teamwork skills.
  • Languages: Proficiency in English is required.

What We Offer

  • Competitive Salary: PKR 150,000 - 250,000 per month based on experience.
  • Benefits: Health insurance, annual bonuses, and performance incentives.
  • Professional Growth: Opportunities for career advancement and skill development.
  • Work Environment: Collaborative and innovative work culture with modern facilities.
  • Flexible Hours: Flexible working hours and remote work options.

Application Deadline: September 30, 2026
