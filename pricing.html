Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

Find answers to common questions about our pricing plans.

Is it really free to post a job?

Yes, you can post your first job for free on Al-Bina Careers. The free plan includes all basic features to help you get started.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade from the Free plan to Premium or Enterprise at any time. Your jobs will automatically get the upgraded features.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards, bank transfers, and mobile payment options for your convenience.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Yes, we offer special pricing for non-profit organizations and educational institutions. Please contact our sales team for details.

How long does it take to post a job?

Posting a job takes less than 5 minutes. Once submitted, your job will be reviewed and published within 24 hours.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription anytime. No long-term contracts required. You'll continue to have access until the end of your billing period.
