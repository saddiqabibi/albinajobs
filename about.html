Connecting Talent with Opportunity Across Pakistan

Al-Bina Careers is Pakistan's most trusted job portal, dedicated to bridging the gap between talented professionals and top employers. Founded with a vision to transform the hiring landscape in Pakistan, we have grown into a comprehensive platform that serves thousands of job seekers and companies across the country.

Our platform offers a seamless experience for both job seekers and employers. Job seekers can discover thousands of verified job listings, while employers can post vacancies and connect with qualified candidates quickly and efficiently.

Our Journey

Company Timeline

The story of Al-Bina Careers from inception to becoming Pakistan's leading job portal.

2020
Al-Bina Careers Founded

Al-Bina Careers was established with a vision to revolutionize the job market in Pakistan. Started with a small team of passionate professionals.

2021
Reached 10,000 Active Users

Within the first year, Al-Bina Careers gained significant traction with over 10,000 job seekers and 500 companies registered on the platform.

2022
Expanded to All Major Cities

Expanded our services to all major cities across Pakistan including Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Multan, and Faisalabad.

2023
Reached 50,000+ Users

Crossed the milestone of 50,000 active job seekers and 2,000+ registered companies. Became one of Pakistan's fastest-growing job portals.

2024
15,000+ Successful Placements

Achieved the milestone of 15,000+ successful career placements. Recognized as Pakistan's most trusted job portal.

2025
Leading Job Portal in Pakistan

Al-Bina Careers continues to lead the job market in Pakistan with 5,000+ live job listings and 2,500+ trusted companies.
