Welcome to Al-Bina Careers! Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our job portal.

1. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our services:

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and password when you create an account.
  • Profile Information: Resume, work experience, education, skills, and professional details.
  • Application Data: Jobs you apply for, messages you send, and your application history.
  • Communication Data: Records of your correspondence with us, including emails and support requests.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device type.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, click patterns, and referral URLs.
  • Location Data: Approximate geographic location based on IP address.
  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device for functionality and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To Provide Services: Match you with job opportunities, process applications, and connect you with employers.
  • To Improve Our Platform: Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience and optimize features.
  • To Communicate: Send job alerts, application updates, promotional materials, and important notices.
  • To Personalize Experience: Tailor job recommendations and content based on your preferences.
  • To Comply with Legal Obligations: Meet regulatory requirements and protect legal rights.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience:

3.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and improve functionality.

3.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality and navigation.
  • Performance Cookies: Collect anonymous data about how visitors use our site to improve performance.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings for future visits.
  • Targeting/Advertising Cookies: Deliver relevant ads and track campaign effectiveness.

3.3 Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect website functionality. Visit our Cookies Policy for more details.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Services

We use Google AdSense and other third-party services to monetize our content:

4.1 Google AdSense

  • Personalized Advertising: Google may use cookies to show ads based on your interests and browsing history.
  • Non-Personalized Ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ad Settings.
  • Third-Party Vendors: Google and other vendors use cookies to serve ads and measure their effectiveness.

4.2 Other Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics: Used for website analytics and to understand user behavior.
  • Social Media Platforms: Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram for social sharing and engagement.

Google AdSense Disclosure: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

5. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: We use SSL/TLS encryption to secure data transmission between your browser and our servers.
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel have access to your personal information on a need-to-know basis.
  • Regular Audits: We conduct periodic security assessments to identify and address vulnerabilities.
  • Data Protection: We store data in secure environments with firewalls and intrusion detection systems.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to maintaining industry-standard safeguards.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for specific periods based on legal and business requirements:

  • Active Users: Data is retained for the duration of your account and for 3 years after account closure.
  • Job Applications: Application data is retained for 5 years for reference and compliance.
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous usage data is retained for up to 26 months.
  • Legal Compliance: We may retain data longer to comply with legal obligations or defend legal claims.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request limitation of how we use your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service provider.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing for direct marketing or legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time for processing based on consent.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we discover that a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete such data.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, plugins, or applications. Clicking on those links may allow third parties to collect or share data about you. We do not control these third-party sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or industry standards. We will notify you of any material changes through:

  • Posting the updated policy on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
  • Sending email notifications to users who have subscribed to updates.
  • Displaying a prominent notice on our website for major changes.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
